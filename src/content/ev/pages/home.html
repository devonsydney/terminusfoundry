3. History & Politics

The history of electric vehicles is rich and fascinating, and illuminates a lot of how we ended up where we are now. The politics of electric vehicles include interests and groups that either do or do not want EVs competing. This world is filled with wild tactics on all sides, such as pre-sales and dealer-less sales, pushing of hype, using social media, taking large stock shorts and pumping of prices, spreading of FUD (fear, uncertainty and doubt) and tongue-in-cheek conspiracy theories.
